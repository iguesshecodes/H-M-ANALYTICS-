# Monthly retention summary

Here is a short monthly update for the retail manager:

As of September 22, 2020, we have seen a slight increase in the share of established revenue at risk, now at 21.5 percent, compared to 20.8 percent at the end of August. This means that 21.5 percent of our revenue is at risk of being lost, but this is not the same as revenue that has already been lost. Our customers who are currently on track for revenue account for 58.2 percent of our established revenue, while those who are slipping account for 20.4 percent. A test on past data suggests that using a contact list ranked by risk times past spend would have caught 49.4 percent of the revenue that was later lost from customers who went quiet. We also know that 42.3 percent of new customers came back within 180 days.

Always read with care: revenue figures are an index, not pounds. The newsletter result is a link, not proof that the newsletter causes buying.
